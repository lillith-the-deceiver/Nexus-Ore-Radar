# Nexus Ore Radar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

## Who maintains Radar

Nexus Ore Radar is maintained by Liliana Boruszewska.
For questions or requests to remove your contributed data, contact
[lilianaboruszewska@gmail.com](mailto:lilianaboruszewska@gmail.com).

## Information Radar uses

Radar uses your existing signed-in Nexus Legacy browser session to read the game
information needed for its features: account identity, fleet and mining reports,
missions, planets, galaxy information and mining belts. It also requests mining
fleet fuel estimates from the game.

Game records, settings and saved locations are stored locally in your browser,
separated by account and season. Radar does not read your unrelated browsing
history or unrelated websites. Your game login credentials are not uploaded to
the maintainer.

## Reports contributed to the maintainer

Free use requires agreement to the automatic contribution of complete **Season 0
pirate-raid reports from mining missions** while Nexus and the extension are
running. Declining the agreement disables Radar.

These reports include all fields supplied by the game, which can include player
and account identifiers, planet and system names, timestamps, fleet compositions,
combat rounds, damage, losses, bonuses and other details contained in the raid
report. Reports are not described as anonymous.

Ordinary mining-yield and breakdown reports, and reports from other seasons,
remain local and are not contributed. A contributed raid report can itself
contain mining or breakdown details; the complete raid report is retained.

## Purpose, storage and access

Contributed reports are used for the further development of Radar. They are
transmitted over HTTPS to the maintainer's Google Apps Script receiver and stored
in Google Drive. Google provides the hosting and storage services.

The maintainer is the only person authorized to access the contributed archive.
It is not intended to be publicly accessible. Reasonable safeguards are used to
protect stored data, but no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed
completely secure.

## Retention and removal

Contributed reports are retained while the maintainer develops or maintains
Radar. If the tool is retired and is no longer developed or maintained, the
maintainer will permanently delete the contributed data, including any
maintainer-held copies and backups.

You may request removal of your contributed reports by emailing
[lilianaboruszewska@gmail.com](mailto:lilianaboruszewska@gmail.com). Include enough
game information to identify the reports, such as your Season 0 player identity
or relevant report IDs. Do not send passwords, session tokens or other login
credentials. The maintainer may ask for additional information needed to identify
the records and confirm the request.

Removal requests cover identifiable contributed records and maintainer-held
copies and backups. Continuing to use Radar can contribute new eligible reports;
disable or uninstall the extension if you want future collection to stop.

Clearing extension storage or uninstalling can remove locally stored records,
but does not automatically delete reports already contributed to the maintainer.
Use the contact above to request their removal.

## Policy changes

This policy will be updated if Radar's data practices change. The date above
identifies the current version.
